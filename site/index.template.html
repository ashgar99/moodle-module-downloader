Moodle Downloader GitHub

Keep your modules
in one click

Download every file from a Moodle module as a single zip, sorted into folders by section.

Download Moodle module

Drag the button to your bookmarks bar.

Ready in four steps

  1. 1

    Show your bookmarks bar

    Press Cmd + Shift + B on a Mac, or Ctrl + Shift + B on Windows.

  2. 2

    Drag the button onto it

    You only need to do this once. It works in Chrome, Brave, Edge and Firefox.

  3. 3

    Open a module

    Log in to Moodle and go to the main page of the module you want to keep.

  4. 4

    Click the bookmark

    A small panel shows the progress, and your zip downloads when it has finished.

  5. That's it

    Each section gets its own folder, in the same order as on Moodle.

Lecture recordings, links to other websites and your own submissions are not included, so save those separately.

Everything runs in your browser using your own login. Nothing is sent anywhere else. Please keep downloaded materials for your own use.
