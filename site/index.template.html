Modzip Modzip

Drag it up to your bookmarks bar

“

Keeping your course materials should not mean clicking into every week and saving each file by hand. Scripts and command-line tools can do it, but most students, from fine art to history, have never used one and should not need to.

The browser extensions that once helped are no longer maintained and have stopped working. Modzip is a single bookmark that does the whole job for you, with nothing to install.

”

As Simple As a Bookmark

Set it up once in a few seconds, then use it on every module you want to keep.

1

Drag it to your bookmarks bar

Drag the Modzip button onto your bookmarks bar. You only need to do this once.

MailLibraryModzipTimetable
2

Open a module on Moodle

Log in as usual and go to the main page of the module you want to save.

Dashboard  /  My modules  /  Studio Practice
3

Click Modzip

Every file downloads into one zip, with a folder for each section in the same order as Moodle.

Studio Practice.zip ├── 01 Welcome ├── 02 Week 1 Introduction │ └── Lecture slides.pdf └── 03 Week 2 Materials └── Readings/

Get Modzip

Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar. It will show as Modzip with a plain globe icon, as browsers do not display icons for bookmarks like this.

Modzip

Can't see your bookmarks bar? Show it with the keys below.

Cmd Shift B on a Mac  ·  Ctrl Shift B on Windows  ·  In Safari, allow downloads if asked

Private

Runs in your browser with your own login. Nothing is sent anywhere else.

Nothing to install

No extension, app or account. It is just a bookmark.

Any Moodle 4 site

Works at any university or college, in Chrome, Edge, Brave, Firefox and Safari on a computer.

Free for good

Open source, with no ads, tracking or paid version.

Lecture recordings, links to other websites and your own submissions are not included. Please keep downloaded materials for your own use.
